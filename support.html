Frequently Asked Questions

Does ElectroSDM require cloud services?

No. ElectroSDM is designed to run completely locally. All communication stays inside your own network and no cloud account is required.

What will the products cost?

Our current target prices are:

• Light Module: €20–45
• Power Supply: €20–40
• ElectroSDM DIN Rail Server: €100–150

Prices may change depending on hardware availability and manufacturing costs.

When will ElectroSDM be available?

Development is still in progress. We expect the first release somewhere within the next 6 to 12 months, depending on development, testing and hardware availability. If you contact us by email, you may be able to receive early access during testing.
