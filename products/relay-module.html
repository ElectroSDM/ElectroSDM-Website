Automatic Backup Communication

ElectroSDM modules primarily communicate over your local Wi-Fi network using MQTT.

If the Wi-Fi network becomes unavailable, the modules are designed to automatically switch to ESP-NOW communication, allowing compatible ElectroSDM modules to continue exchanging essential commands locally.

When the Wi-Fi connection is restored, communication automatically returns to the normal MQTT network without user interaction.

This design helps keep connected wall switches and automation functions available, even during temporary Wi-Fi interruptions.

🔋 Power Supply Recommendation

ElectroSDM Light Modules require a stable 5V DC power supply. You are free to use any high-quality 5V power supply that can provide enough current for your installation.

Recommended ElectroSDM Power Supply

For the easiest installation, we recommend using an ElectroSDM DIN Rail Power Supply. It is designed and tested specifically for ElectroSDM installations and makes wiring inside the electrical cabinet much easier.
