About ElectroSDM

Who We Are

ElectroSDM is an independent one-man project developed in Belgium. Every PCB, software application and website is designed from the ground up to provide affordable, reliable and privacy-focused smart home automation.

Our Mission

Today's smart home market is dominated by expensive ecosystems that depend on cloud services and subscriptions. ElectroSDM aims to provide a powerful alternative that is completely local, affordable and under your control.

Local First

Every installation can run entirely on your own hardware, whether that's a Windows PC, Linux server, NAS, Mini PC or the optional ElectroSDM DIN Rail Server.

Your Choice

Software updates are always optional. You decide when your installation receives updates, giving you full control over stability and new features.

Privacy Policy

No Mandatory Cloud

ElectroSDM is designed to operate completely locally. Internet access is never required for daily use.

Your Data

Your smart home data stays inside your own network. ElectroSDM never sells or shares your personal information.

Email Privacy

If you contact us by email, your email address is only used to reply to your message.

Our Promise

No advertisements, no hidden telemetry, no unnecessary tracking and no mandatory online account.
