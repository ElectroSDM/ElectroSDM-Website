About ElectroSDM

Built around privacy, reliability and complete local control.

Who We Are

ElectroSDM is an independent one-man project developed in Belgium. Every PCB, software application and website is designed from the ground up to create professional smart home automation that is affordable and easy to use.

Our Mission

Today's smart home market is dominated by expensive closed ecosystems. ElectroSDM was created to offer a powerful alternative that focuses on freedom, reliability and fair pricing without unnecessary cloud services.

Local First

Every installation can run entirely on your own hardware. Whether you choose a Windows PC, Linux server, Mini PC, NAS or the optional ElectroSDM DIN Rail Server, your data always stays under your control.

Your Choice

Firmware updates are always optional. You decide when your system is updated, giving you complete control over stability and new features.

Privacy Policy

Privacy is not a feature. It is a foundation.

No Mandatory Cloud

ElectroSDM works completely locally. Internet access is never required for everyday operation.

Your Data

Personal information is never sold or shared with third parties. Your smart home data remains inside your own network.

Email Contact

If you contact ElectroSDM by email, your email address is only used to reply to your message.

Our Promise

No advertising network, no unnecessary tracking, no hidden telemetry and no mandatory online account.
