About ElectroSDM

Built around privacy, reliability and complete local control.

🇧🇪

Who We Are

ElectroSDM is an independent one-man project developed in Belgium. Every product is designed from the ground up with one goal: making professional smart home automation affordable for everyone.

🎯

Our Mission

Today's smart home market is dominated by expensive closed ecosystems. ElectroSDM focuses on freedom, fair pricing and long-term reliability.

🏠

Local First

Run your installation on your own Windows PC, Linux server, Mini PC, NAS or the optional ElectroSDM DIN Rail Server.

⚙️

Your Choice

Updates are always optional. You choose when your smart home receives new features.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy always comes first.

🔒

No Mandatory Cloud

ElectroSDM is designed to operate completely locally. Internet access is never required for normal daily operation.

🏠

Your Data Stays Yours

Your smart home data remains inside your own network. ElectroSDM never uploads your data to external servers.

✉️

Email Privacy

If you contact us by email, your address is only used to reply to your message and nothing else.

🛡️

Our Promise

No advertisements, no unnecessary tracking, no selling of personal information and no hidden telemetry.
