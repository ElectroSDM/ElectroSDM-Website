Our Mission

Creating a better alternative for modern smart homes.

ElectroSDM started with one simple goal: create smart home products that are affordable, reliable and completely controlled by the user.

Today's smart home market is largely dominated by expensive closed ecosystems. Many systems require cloud services, subscriptions or proprietary hardware.

ElectroSDM takes a different approach.

Every installation can run locally on your own Windows PC, Linux server, NAS, Mini PC or the optional ElectroSDM DIN Rail Server.

Software updates are always optional. You decide when your installation is updated.

We believe smart homes should belong to their owners, not to cloud providers.
