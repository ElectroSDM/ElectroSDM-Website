About ElectroSDM

Learn more about our mission and vision.

Who We Are

ElectroSDM is currently an independent one-man project developed in Belgium. Every product, PCB, software application and website is designed and developed with one goal: creating a modern smart home platform that everyone can afford.


Our Mission

Today's smart home market is largely dominated by expensive closed ecosystems. Many systems depend on cloud services, subscriptions or limited compatibility.

ElectroSDM aims to offer an affordable alternative without compromising on quality, performance or reliability.


Freedom & Local Control

Every ElectroSDM installation can run completely locally. You choose where your server runs:

  • Your own Windows PC
  • A Linux server
  • A Mini PC
  • A NAS
  • The optional ElectroSDM DIN Rail Server

Software updates are always optional. You decide when your system is updated, giving you complete control over your installation.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy comes first.

Local First

ElectroSDM does not require cloud services for normal operation. Your devices can communicate entirely inside your own local network.


Personal Information

Data Collection
