About ElectroSDM

ElectroSDM is a modern smart home platform designed around one simple idea: your home should always remain under your control. Every module communicates locally, your data stays inside your own network, and cloud services are never required for daily operation. Whether you install one module or build an entire smart home, ElectroSDM grows together with your installation.

Designed & Developed in Belgium. Professional local smart home automation focused on privacy, reliability and complete local control.
